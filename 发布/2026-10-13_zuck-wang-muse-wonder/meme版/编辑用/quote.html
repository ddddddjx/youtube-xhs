开支票那会儿，他们三个人的原话

Cheeky Pint 播客，John Collison 对谈 Mark Zuckerberg、Alexandr Wang
Zuckerberg · 开场
We haven't seen the models yet. We're going to write this check for like a hundred billion dollars or $200 billion. I'm like, "Alex, you sure this is going to work?"
模型一个还没见到，一两千亿美元的支票就要开了。我问 Alex，你确定这能成？
Wang · 讲去年底夏威夷的高管会
Susan pulled me aside many times and was like, if this doesn't work… it's going to be very very bad in financial terms. This model has to work.
CFO Susan 好几次把我拉到一边：要是不成，财务上会非常非常糟，这个模型必须成。
Wang · 讲为什么做不了小步快跑
It's like the opposite of lean startup… you cannot do that in AI. We've been investing into these data centers for years and years.
这是 lean startup 的反面，AI 里做不到先验证再加码，数据中心已经投了好几年。
