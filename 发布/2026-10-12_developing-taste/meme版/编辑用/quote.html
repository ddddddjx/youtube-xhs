《Developing Taste》里的三句话

Emil Kowalski · emilkowal.ski · 原文摘录
In a world of scarcity, we treasure tools. In a world of abundance, we treasure taste.
稀缺的年代，人们看重工具；富足的年代，人们看重品味。（Anu Atluru）
Simply shipping a product that works is no longer enough, everyone can do that, especially now with AI.
只交付一个能用的产品已经不够了，谁都做得到，有了 AI 更是如此。
It's commonly mistaken as personal preference, but it's more than that — it's a trained instinct.
品味常被当成个人偏好，其实它是一种练出来的直觉。
