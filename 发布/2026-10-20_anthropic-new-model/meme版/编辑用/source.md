## 主帖内容

ANTHROPIC 🔥: According to The Information, Anthropic will release a new model this month, along with Gemini 4 Carbon and a new Terra model from OpenAI. 

> "The unreleased model is especially good at discerning a person’s underlying intentions and goals even if they don’t give the model much information."

> "The Anthropic model is also better at thinking outside of the box rather than regurgitating common sense advice or solutions."

> "It’s not clear which model family Anthropic will fall into, such as Fable or Opus."

Thinking outside of the box is a huge limitation these days. If we get a model that can do it reliably, we'll be properly transitioning into the "Innovators" stage. 

The line about "discerning underlying intentions" is a bit scary, though, given all the guardrails.
